# Project Overview

## Purpose

The Hattrick Steptrading Application is designed to help Hattrick players track, analyze, and optimize their steptrading profitability. Steptrading in Hattrick involves buying players, training them to improve their skills, and selling them at higher market values. This application provides comprehensive tools to monitor trading performance, calculate profits, and make data-driven decisions about player investments.

The primary goal is to transform the often manual and spreadsheet-based tracking that Hattrick players currently use into a modern, user-friendly web application that provides deeper insights and automated calculations.

## Target Audience

### Primary Users
- **Active Hattrick Players**: Players who regularly engage in buying and selling players as part of their team management strategy
- **Steptrading Specialists**: Players who focus primarily on player trading as their main strategy for team improvement and financial growth
- **Data-Driven Managers**: Players who want detailed analytics and historical data to improve their trading decisions

### User Characteristics
- **Experience Level**: Intermediate to advanced Hattrick players (typically playing for 6+ months)
- **Technical Comfort**: Basic to intermediate computer users comfortable with web applications
- **Trading Volume**: Players managing 5-50+ player transactions per season
- **Geographic Distribution**: Global user base, primarily in Europe, South America, and Asia where Hattrick is most popular

### User Motivations
- Improve trading profitability through better data tracking
- Reduce time spent on manual calculations and record-keeping
- Gain insights into market trends and optimal trading strategies
- Make more informed decisions about player investments

## Key Features

### Core Trading Management
- **Player Portfolio Management**: Track current and historical players with detailed attributes
- **Transaction Recording**: Log buy/sell transactions with comprehensive details (price, date, circumstances)
- **Profit/Loss Calculations**: Automated calculation of trading profits including training costs
- **Training Progress Tracking**: Monitor skill improvements and training investments

### Analytics and Reporting
- **Performance Dashboard**: Overview of trading performance with key metrics
- **Portfolio Analytics**: ROI analysis, success rates by player type/position
- **Market Trend Analysis**: Historical price trends and market insights
- **Comparative Analysis**: Performance comparison across different trading strategies

### Data Management
- **Import/Export Capabilities**: Support for common data formats used by Hattrick players
- **Backup and Sync**: Secure data storage with export options
- **Search and Filtering**: Advanced filtering options for players and transactions
- **Historical Data Retention**: Long-term storage of trading history for trend analysis

### User Experience Features
- **Responsive Design**: Optimized for desktop and mobile use
- **Intuitive Interface**: User-friendly forms and dashboards designed for Hattrick terminology
- **Customizable Views**: Personalized dashboards and report configurations
- **Notification System**: Alerts for market opportunities or portfolio changes

## Business Context

### Problem Statement
Hattrick steptrading currently relies on manual tracking methods:
- Players use spreadsheets or paper records that are error-prone and time-consuming
- Lack of standardized profit calculations leads to inconsistent analysis
- No centralized platform for tracking market trends and trading patterns
- Difficulty in analyzing long-term trading performance and strategy effectiveness

### Market Opportunity
- **Large User Base**: Hattrick has over 1 million registered users globally
- **Active Trading Community**: Significant portion of players engage in regular trading
- **Underserved Market**: Limited specialized tools available for Hattrick trading analysis
- **Growth Potential**: Opportunity to expand to related football management games

### Competitive Advantage
- **Hattrick-Specific**: Designed specifically for Hattrick mechanics and terminology
- **Comprehensive Analytics**: More detailed analysis than generic portfolio trackers
- **Community Focus**: Built by and for the Hattrick community
- **Modern Technology**: Web-based solution accessible from anywhere

### Business Value
- **User Efficiency**: Reduce time spent on manual tracking by 70-80%
- **Decision Quality**: Improve trading decisions through better data insights
- **Community Building**: Create a platform for sharing trading strategies and market insights
- **Educational Value**: Help new players learn effective steptrading techniques
